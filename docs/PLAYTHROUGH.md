# Complete playthrough — Dawn returns their names

**Spoilers:** this guide reveals every observation answer, the hidden-room code, the case conclusion, and the fullest ending. For help without solutions, start with the [controls](CONTROLS.md) and in-game graduated hints.

This route follows the story rules and interface labels in the first playable **1.0.0** release. It works with authored dialogue; free-text AI questions are optional.

## Before you begin

Start a new story, watch or skip the opening, and enter through the Entrance foyer. If you already have a save, Settings offers **Export save** and **Restart**. Progress and conversations save in this browser; there is no cloud save.

Use WASD/arrows to move, Shift to run, C to crouch/stand, and E when close to an object or resident. Drag to rotate the camera; R restores its original angle. Use **M** for the floor plan and **I / B / Tab** for the investigator's bag. The map shows your position and does not move you. Close the bag with Escape or its close button before walking, then press movement again; opening a window releases held movement and temporarily pauses residents.

An inspected physical object is recorded immediately and opens the bag. A memory needs an additional recording step:

1. Approach its resident and press E. Choose **Show: [the relevant evidence]**, then **✧ Step into a memory** / **✧ เข้าสู่ความทรงจำ**.
2. Select **moment 2** using Next or the numbered moment controls on the scene.
3. Open the bag with I, or select **Compare details** / **เทียบรายละเอียด**. Choose the observation answer below.
4. Press **✧ Record this detail** / **✧ บันทึกรายละเอียดนี้**. Solving the observation alone does not add the evidence.
5. Return to the memory view if needed, then choose **Return to the present** / **กลับไปคุยในปัจจุบัน**. Close the bag before walking onward.

Wrong answers are recoverable. Revisit moment 2, retry, and record the detail; missing a moment never destroys evidence.

## Ground floor: evidence 1–6

Follow open doorways through the central Grand stair hall. Furniture and walls block movement; walk around them rather than cutting through the room.

| Step | Where and what to do | Answer / new evidence | Total |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Enter the **Dining room**. Approach the standing clock and press E to inspect the **Clock repair tag** / **ป้ายซ่อมนาฬิกา**. Close the bag. | Clock repair tag | 1/14 |
| 2 | Enter the **Kitchen**, through its serving doorway or the hall. Inspect the **Serving ledger** / **สมุดเสิร์ฟอาหาร** on the preparation table. | Serving ledger | 2/14 |
| 3 | In the Kitchen, talk to **Lamai / ป้าละไม**. Show the Serving ledger and enter her memory. Solve moment 2 and explicitly record it. | **At the stairs / หน้าบันได** → **The key Mira requested / กุญแจที่มีราขอ** | 3/14 |
| 4 | Return to the present, close the bag, and go to the **Clock workshop**. Talk to **Arun · Father / พ่อ · อรุณ**, show the Clock repair tag, and solve/record moment 2. | **19:00** → **The instruction after the chime / คำสั่งหลังเสียงระฆัง** | 4/14 |
| 5 | Go to the **Music room**. Inspect **The unsent warning / คำเตือนที่ไม่เคยส่ง** in the piano book. | The unsent warning | 5/14 |
| 6 | Talk to **Lin · Sister / น้องสาว · ลิน** in the Music room. Show The unsent warning, enter her memory, and solve/record moment 2. | **Ordering name and signature / ชื่อผู้สั่งและลายเซ็น** → **The physician's receipt / ใบรับของของหมอ** | 6/14 |

The clock was one hour fast. Lin delivered the envelope, but the ordering name and signature identify who ordered its contents.

## Second floor: evidence 7–10

Return to the Grand stair hall. Walk up the grand staircase's **west flight**, follow the rear turn landing across to the **east flight**, and continue to the **Upper landing**. These are physical stairs: keep walking and turn on the landing; no stair interaction button is required. West/east refer to the floor plan, so rotate or reset the camera when needed.

| Step | Where and what to do | Answer / new evidence | Total |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 7 | From the Upper landing, enter the **Infirmary**. Inspect the **Incomplete medicine register / ทะเบียนยาที่ขาดหน้า** on the register desk. | Incomplete medicine register | 7/14 |
| 8 | Talk to **Dr. Saran / หมอ · ศรัณย์**. Show the Incomplete medicine register; solve and record moment 2. | **Matching signature / ลายเซ็นเดียวกัน** → **The page Saran replaced / หน้าที่หมอเปลี่ยน** | 8/14 |
| 9 | Enter the **Guest bedroom**. Inspect **Nara's marked photograph / ภาพที่นาราขีดไว้** on the desk. | Nara's marked photograph | 9/14 |
| 10 | Talk to **Nara · Survivor / ผู้รอดชีวิต · นารา**. Show her photograph; solve and record moment 2. | **3 · 1 · 2** → **The passage and knocks / ทางลอดและเสียงเคาะ** | 10/14 |

Return to the present and close the bag. Descend the grand stairs in reverse: east flight, rear landing, west flight, then the ground-floor hall. Nara's account establishes the passage and knock sequence; the hidden panel also requires Somchai's corroborating evidence.

## Basement and hidden room: evidence 11–14

Go back to the Kitchen. Walk down the service staircase's **west flight**, cross its rear landing, then follow the **east flight** into the **Cellar passage**. Use the same movement controls through both turns.

| Step | Where and what to do | Answer / new evidence | Total |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 11 | Enter the **Old mortuary** from the Cellar passage. Inspect the **Twelve-name entry register / ทะเบียนสิบสองชื่อ** on its table. | Twelve-name entry register | 11/14 |
| 12 | Enter **Boiler and iron door**. Inspect the **Iron-door service log / บันทึกประตูเหล็ก** on the caretaker's desk. | Iron-door service log | 12/14 |
| 13 | Talk to **Somchai · Caretaker / คนดูแล · สมชาย**. Show the Iron-door service log; solve and record moment 2. | **Outside / ด้านนอก** → **The outside bar / คานด้านนอก** | 13/14 |
| 14 | Return to the present, close the bag, and approach the boiler room's hidden panel. Press E, enter **312**, and select **Open the panel / เปิดผนัง**. Walk through the opened wall into **The sealed room** and inspect **Original order and death list / คำสั่งต้นฉบับและรายชื่อผู้ตาย**. | Original order and death list | 14/14 |

The sealed room is entered through the boiler wall; there is no doorway from the Cellar passage. If the panel says **One memory is not enough**, check that both **The passage and knocks** and **The outside bar** appear in the bag. Correct observation answers without pressing Record leave the gate locked.

## Reconstruct the evening

Open the bag and choose **Reconstruct the evening / ประกอบเหตุการณ์**. Assign these real times:

| Event | Time |
|---|---|
| The doctor's delivery / หมอสั่งของ | 18:45 |
| Envelope reaches Mira / ซองถึงมีรา | 18:55 |
| Dinner and the chime / อาหารและระฆัง | 19:00 |
| Door instruction / คำสั่งปิดประตู | 19:05 |
| Mira descends to help / มีราลงไปช่วย | 19:20 |
| The outside bar closes / คานปิดจากด้านนอก | 19:25 |
| Nara escapes / นาราหนีออก | 19:30 |
| Internal death record / ทะเบียนผู้ตาย | 19:40 |

For the two evidence questions, select:

- **What proves the chime was not at 20:00?** → **Clock repair tag / ป้ายซ่อมนาฬิกา**.
- **What identifies who placed the order before the envelope was passed?** → **The physician's receipt / ใบรับของของหมอ**.

Press **Check the timeline / ตรวจลำดับเหตุการณ์**. The conclusion form follows when the timeline is correct.

## Make the conclusion

| Question | Choose |
|---|---|
| Who signed the closure order? | **Saran / ศรัณย์** |
| What was this crime? | **Murder to conceal fraud and patient abuse / ฆาตกรรมเพื่อปกปิดทุจริตและทำร้ายผู้ป่วย** |
| Why did Mira descend? | **Rescued Nara, then returned for the others / ช่วยนารา แล้วกลับไปช่วยคนอื่น** |

Press **Confirm your conclusion / ยืนยันข้อสรุป**. The last-chapter window opens. If you close it, reopen the bag and select **Prepare the last chapter / เตรียมบทสุดท้าย**.

## Prepare all three actions and choose the fullest ending

Use the preparation buttons in the last-chapter window:

| Preparation | Resident | Correct choice |
|---|---|---|
| **Signed admission / คำให้การรับผิด** | Arun | **I shut the door. I will testify and surrender originals. / ผมปิดประตู จะให้การและส่งมอบบันทึกเดิม** |
| **Preserve originals / รักษาต้นฉบับ** | Lin | **Preserve originals and testimony. Do not rewrite events. / เก็บต้นฉบับพร้อมคำให้การ ไม่แก้เหตุการณ์** |
| **Protect the survivor / คุ้มครองผู้รอดชีวิต** | Lamai | **Testify somewhere safe when ready. No forced return below. / ให้การในที่ปลอดภัยเมื่อพร้อม ไม่ต้องลงไปอีก** |

After each answer, choose **Continue preparing / กลับไปเตรียมบทสุดท้าย**. All three preparations should show a checkmark. Select **Dawn returns their names / รุ่งเช้าที่คืนชื่อให้ผู้ตาย**, then **Confirm / ยืนยัน**.

You submit the originals, admissions, and protected testimony. Saran's case reopens, the dead are identified, and Nara remains safe. This ending brings justice and remembrance; Mira and the other victims are not resurrected.
